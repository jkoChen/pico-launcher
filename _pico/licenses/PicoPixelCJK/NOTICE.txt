PicoPixelCJK is a modified bitmap font derived from Fusion Pixel Font
(2026.09.25), Copyright (c) 2022 TakWolf.

Source: https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font
Pinned source commit: 6c88c8ec0f16f05e06663890a043ecbc81d448ae

Changes: BMP CJK and punctuation subset of the native 10px and 12px
monospaced zh_hans variants, converted to Pico Launcher NFT2 format.
Glyph outlines retain their original native pixel grid without resampling.
Source archive and font hashes are recorded in tools/fonts/manifest.json
in the launcher source repository.

The derived fonts are distributed under SIL Open Font License 1.1.
See OFL.txt for the full license. The LICENSES directory preserves all
upstream notices supplied in the two source font archives, including
Ark Pixel, BoutiqueBitmap9x9, Cubic 11, and Galmuri.
